# Now: What happens AFTER the doctor gets the case?

This is the next major piece we need to design.

I would **not** make:

```text
Doctor reviews
 ↓
Prescription
 ↓
Case ends
```

the default model.

A doctor case should be treated as a **clinical episode**, and the doctor decides what happens next.

---

# 1. The Doctor Case Lifecycle

I'd structure it roughly like:

```text
Clinical Event
      ↓
Risk Assessment
      ↓
Doctor Required
      ↓
Doctor Case Created
      ↓
Doctor Assigned
      ↓
Doctor Reviews Case
      ↓
Clinical Decision
      ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                     │
│ What should happen next?            │
│                                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Then there are several possible outcomes.

---

# 2. Scenario A — No Treatment Required

This is an important case people often forget.

Example:

```text
Patient
 ↓
Abnormal but non-dangerous vitals
 ↓
Doctor reviews
 ↓
Determines patient is stable
 ↓
No treatment required
```

Doctor can write:

> Continue monitoring. No medication required.

Then:

```text
Doctor resolves case
 ↓
Case CLOSED
```

The Visit can continue/complete normally.

### Requirement

Doctor needs:

* Clinical assessment
* Doctor remarks
* Resolution
* Case closure

---

# 3. Scenario B — Prescription Only

This is the one you mentioned.

```text
Doctor reviews
 ↓
Diagnosis / assessment
 ↓
Prescription
 ↓
Patient receives medication instructions
 ↓
Case resolved
```

For example:

```text
Diagnosis:
Mild infection

Prescription:
Medication X
Dose
Frequency
Duration
```

Then:

```text
Case
 ↓
RESOLVED
```

### But there is an important distinction:

**Prescription does not necessarily mean case ends immediately.**

The doctor might say:

> Start medication and monitor for 3 days.

That becomes a follow-up case.


# 6. Your Idea: Existing Nurse Continues

Yes — I think this is very important.

Suppose:


# 24. The Status Model Is Going to Be Important

I'd recommend we eventually distinguish **Case status** from **Care Plan status** and **Visit status**.

For example:

### Clinical Case

```text
OPEN
ASSIGNED
IN_REVIEW
AWAITING_INFORMATION
TREATMENT_ACTIVE
REFERRED
ESCALATED
RESOLVED
CLOSED
```

### Care Plan

```text
DRAFT
ACTIVE
PAUSED
COMPLETED
CANCELLED
```

### Visit

You already have:

```text
SCHEDULED
ACCEPTED
IN_PROGRESS
COMPLETED
```

Don't merge these into one status.

That's important because:

```text
Case = RESOLVED
Care Plan = ACTIVE
Visit = COMPLETED
```

can legitimately happen simultaneously.

## One architectural decision I think we should make next

Before giving Antigravity a prompt, I would settle one thing:

### **Care Plan vs Clinical Case**

I recommend:

> **Clinical Case = a doctor's clinical problem/review episode.**

> **Care Plan = the ongoing treatment/monitoring strategy created by the doctor.**

So:

```text
Vitals abnormal
 ↓
Clinical Case
 ↓
Doctor reviews
 ↓
Creates Care Plan
 ↓
Clinical Case can be resolved
 ↓
Care Plan remains ACTIVE
 ↓
Nurse performs recurring Visits
 ↓
Monitoring
 ↓
Doctor follow-up
 ↓
Care Plan completed
```

That distinction will prevent a **lot of architectural confusion later**, especially when we connect prescriptions, recurring visits, risk assessments, follow-ups, and medical records.
