## My assessment

**You are much further along than the raw PBD makes it look.**

The **core Healix clinical/business workflow is already substantially implemented and E2E validated**. The biggest missing piece is **not another basic CRUD module**—it is turning the existing rule-based Clinical Intelligence layer into the actual **AI/ML + RAG system promised by the PBD**.

After that, **Payment/Escrow** is the biggest business-feature gap, followed by **Analytics/Reporting** and then the more advanced **Compliance/Fraud** features.

> **Full PBD product: ~50–60% complete**

Those percentages are **rough scope estimates**, not measured code-coverage percentages. The reason the full-PBD number is lower is that Modules 8, 12, 13 and 15 contain a lot of functionality that has not been built yet.
